# Phase 3 Application Intelligence — Batch 02

## Batch objective
Research the ten additional SKUs promoted into the 20-product launch catalogue. This batch prioritizes recent demand, customer breadth, application clarity and exact part-number searchability over the original V1 rank alone.

## Promoted launch SKUs
- CX107P405-TWD
- CX110C005-TWD
- SP314C002-TWD
- IT1071028B-TWD
- SP318C003-TWD
- 6926E-TWD
- O5499E-TWD
- 5659P-TWD
- A5579D-TWD
- CX108P305-TWD

## Research findings

### CX107P405-TWD
- Exact active Twin Disc CX107P assembly.
- Family applications: mud, manure, injection and irrigation pumps; oil & gas; flood control; wood chippers; tub grinders; conveyors; compressors; blowers; fans; grain dryers.
- No exact OEM cross-reference for P405 found in current public sources.
- Prospect from the application family, but do not claim an exact machine fit without additional evidence.

### CX110C005-TWD
- CX110 clutch/service component.
- Palmer Johnson publishes exact OEM cross-reference **Vermeer 603643003**.
- The CX110 family is used across pump, forestry, conveyor, compressor, sewer-truck and grain-dryer applications.
- SAP's item description says it replaces CX110C002. That replacement relationship is internal evidence and should be verified before website publication.

### SP314C002-TWD
- Palmer Johnson parts list description: **SP314 3.25TA M MD1 BB S697**.
- SP314P family applications: sand dredges, irrigation pumps, crane/hoisting, metal shredders, recycling equipment, jaw crushers, compressors.
- Earthsaver Parts maps SP314C002 to **Bandit 900-1914-95 / 900-6917-10, Morbark 24542-628 and Vermeer 156154001**.
- These OEM numbers are strong specialist-source evidence, but should receive one final verification before public compatibility copy.

### IT1071028B-TWD
- Official Twin Disc drawings identify **IT1071028B** as the SAE #3 / 11.5-inch flywheel input assembly used on multiple AM220 pump-drive BOMs.
- Palmer Johnson lists AM220 as a two-pad pump drive with maximum input power of **355 kW / 476 hp at 1:1 and 3200 rpm**.
- Best prospect categories: drilling equipment, hydraulic pump-drive OEMs, mobile hydraulic machinery and industrial hydraulic service.
- Existing-customer evidence can guide prospecting, but customer identity is not equipment-fit evidence.

### SP318C003-TWD
- Palmer Johnson parts list: **SP318 4.30TA F MD1 BB S777**.
- Exact OEM mappings published by Palmer Johnson: **Terex 70012123, Vermeer 154820001, Bandit 900-6914-08, Trelan 00CLU-398**.
- Modern Equipment Parts explicitly maps Bandit 900-6914-08 to SP318C003 for an SP318S001 Twin Disc clutch.
- Note: an aftermarket specialist also assigns certain Morbark numbers to C003, but those conflict with Palmer Johnson's mapping to other SP318 clutch BOMs. Do not publish the conflicting Morbark numbers.

### 6926E-TWD
- Drive ring for Twin Disc SP318 / PO318 family.
- Bandit Beast 2680 manual explicitly lists **6926E — Ring, Driving** in its Twin Disc **SP318SBO** PTO.
- Bandit aftermarket mapping: **900-1915-13**, with a retailer replacement note to **900-6955-20**.
- Excellent long-tail SEO and outbound part because the equipment, PTO family and OEM number can all be connected.

### O5499E-TWD
- Palmer Johnson parts list: **Friction Disc Sin**.
- Authorized Twin Disc dealer Diesel Parts Direct states O5499E can be used on **IBF314** PTOs.
- IB314/IBF314 is a high-inertia PTO family commonly used with **vertical-shaft impact crushers, tub grinders and rock crushers**.
- Strong prospect pools: aggregate crushing, demolition/recycling, grinder service and PTO rebuilders.

### 5659P-TWD
- Palmer Johnson parts list: **Friction Disc MD1**.
- Authorized Twin Disc dealer lists 5659P as an **IBF314 clutch plate**.
- WPT cross-reference sheet maps W14-07-901 to Twin Disc **5659A / 5659P**.
- Excellent recurring service component for crusher/tub-grinder and IBF314 maintenance markets.

### A5579D-TWD
- Palmer Johnson parts list: **Friction Disc MD1**.
- Authorized Twin Disc dealer lists A5579D as a **solid drive plate for SP111** PTOs.
- Secondary aftermarket sources also map A5579D to SP211/SP211HP3, but that broader compatibility should remain secondary until independently verified.
- Very strong launch candidate because it combines high recurring demand with exact part-number search behavior.

### CX108P305-TWD
- Exact active CX108P assembly.
- Palmer Johnson parts list description: **C108HP3 MD3 BB 305 SB**.
- Family applications: mud/manure/injection/irrigation pumps, oil & gas, flood control, wood chippers, tub grinders, conveyors, compressors, blowers, fans and grain dryers.
- No exact OEM cross-reference for P305 found in current public sources.

## Buyer-pattern insight
The launch catalogue is now intentionally balanced across:
1. complete PTO assemblies;
2. exact OEM-cross-reference clutch packs;
3. high-frequency friction/service parts;
4. drive rings with specific equipment evidence;
5. a pump-drive input component that opens a drilling/hydraulics prospect segment.

This is preferable to simply taking ranks 1–20 from the V1 score because some higher-ranked products had little or no recent demand.
