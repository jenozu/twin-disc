## Current Milestone

**Phase 1 — SAP Product Intelligence**

Goal: identify the first 20–50 Twin Disc products to prioritize for the store.

## Immediate Sequence

1. Run SAP Query 00 to confirm the exact Twin Disc manufacturer record.
2. Run 5-year, 10-year, and 12-month product-demand queries.
3. Export the complete sales ledger and item master locally.
4. Identify the correct SAP retail/list price source.
5. Build product opportunity scores.
6. Select the initial 20–50 SKUs.

## Working Principle

Do not build a giant catalogue first. Let observed demand, economics, and identifiable applications determine what gets listed and marketed.
