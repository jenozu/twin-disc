## Recommended initial geographic architecture
1. **One truthful Ontario service-area page or site-wide shipping/inquiry explanation** after geographical servicing/fulfilment is confirmed. Content: parts inquiry process, available categories, identification requirements, transit/coverage constraints and RFQ. Do not imply physical presence.
2. **Two differentiated candidate region pages only after business approval:** Hamilton/Burlington (port + heavy manufacturing), Waterloo Region (industrial machinery/PTO). Distinct FAQs, relevant model/part links, actual case/applications, no fabricated projects.
3. **Niagara, Durham, Georgian Bay, Windsor-Essex and Thunder Bay** stay in the research/backlog until search signals, owner coverage and unique content are proven.
4. Use `Service`/repair city pages **only** if actual technician/workshop capability and territory are verified; otherwise link to independent parts/RFQ not fake service.
5. Google Business Profile requires real eligible customer-facing presence; do not create fake branch/location listings. Avoid doorway pages, copied descriptions, photos from unlicensed sources and unverifiable local testimonials.

## Evidence strength and needed research
**Known:** ports, manufacturing and marina industries demonstrably exist. **Unknown:** regional Twin Disc installed-base size, customers, order lead-time, service capacity, local search volume/rankings and which business actually fulfils orders. **SEO conclusion:** these are thematic opportunities, not proven ROI. Validate with Ontario-filtered GSC query pages, Google Trends (relative only), SERP inspection, suppliers/customer interviews and consenting buyer requests.
