# Phase 2–3 Workflow

## Goal
Turn the SAP shortlist into a verified product/application database that can drive both e-commerce pages and prospecting.

## Phase 2 — Product Family Classification
For every shortlisted SKU capture:
- SAP SKU
- clean Twin Disc part number
- normalized family
- Twin Disc product family/model
- complete assembly vs service component
- current/legacy/superseded status
- exact configuration when documented
- confidence level

## Phase 3 — Equipment & Application Intelligence
For every shortlisted SKU capture:
- family applications
- exact OEM cross-references
- exact equipment-model evidence
- engine compatibility only when documented
- torque/HP/speed specifications only from reliable sources
- related parts/assemblies
- service/rebuild relationships
- prospect types
- search keywords
- source URLs
- confidence

## Evidence hierarchy
1. Twin Disc official product page/drawing/manual
2. OEM operator/parts manual
3. Authorized Twin Disc distributor
4. Reputable equipment dealer/service documentation
5. Secondary marketplace/trade data only as supporting evidence

## Publication rule
A website compatibility statement must be backed by Exact evidence. Family evidence may be used for category/educational copy, but must not be presented as exact equipment fit.

## Batch method
Research 10 SKUs at a time. After each batch:
1. update product master;
2. update application intelligence;
3. identify OEM cross-reference keywords;
4. identify prospect segments;
5. flag supersessions/conflicts;
6. promote verified products toward the launch catalogue.
