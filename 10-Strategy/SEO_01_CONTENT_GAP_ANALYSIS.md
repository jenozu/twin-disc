**Prepared:** 2026-10-19. **Status:** Research briefs only; proposed URLs do not exist in proven production. Sources identify product and application relevance, NOT search demand/volume. No article or product is automatically authorized by inclusion.

## Strategic gaps in traditional industrial product catalogs

| Gap | Distinct content opportunity | Restriction |
|---|---|---|
| Legacy gear variants | MG-506-1/A serial, BOM and reduction-ratio identification | No assumed replacements; do not substitute MG-5065A specification |
| Discontinued service assemblies | Transparency and manual review for SP211P304 and SP111P340 | Both remain HOLD; PX service references not established as drop-in |
| Exact OEM-number lookups | One SKU page capturing sourced Vermeer/Bandit/Morbark/Terex/Trelan numbers | Only Phase4 Yes within exact scope; Review crossrefs excluded |
| Unknown-model buyers | Symptom-to-ID-to-family-to-part-to-RFQ path | Preview RFQ does not submit; staff/backend required |
| Application context | Bandit Beast 3680 versus 3680 Track distinction; pump and compressor duties | Family uses never become exact-fit |
| Ontario context | Unique port/manufacturing buyer workflows and real delivery promises | No doorway/location pages or unoffered service claims |
