## Intent patterns from the sampled sites
1. **Model/BOM search** wins with a dedicated technical model page (Palmer Johnson CX110P, Diesel Parts Direct MG-506).
2. **Exact part number** fits a canonical SKU landing page, especially where verified OEM references are exposed correctly.
3. **Legacy marine discovery** benefits from repair/manual links, suffix comparisons and a fallback enquiry when an individual internal part number is unknown.
4. **Service searches** are served by providers who substantiate actual technicians, workshops and support; TWD must defer such pages until its capabilities exist.
5. **Commercial CTA** typically appears at the product/model page rather than forcing navigation back to a generic contact page; qualify with part/BOM/serial as available.
6. **Canadian location differentiation** must come from true shipping/coverage details and specific local industries, not city-swap pages.

## Content architecture comparison

| Legacy manuals | Diesel Parts Direct MG-506 index | Identification checklist + BOM/suffix decision tree; official-source comparison after review |
| Aftermarket part shopping | Foley Engines SP211 products | Separate “reported aftermarket replacement” from authorized OEM cross-reference |
| Service / emergency | Palmer Johnson, Great Lakes Power | Capture **urgent parts enquiries**, not fictional repair/24h dispatch |
| Canadian shopping | Diesel Pro | Ontario shipping availability, customs/taxes and response times only when operationally confirmed |

## Confidence and limitations
This is **competitor feature sampling**, not an exhaustive crawl or ranking-gap analysis. Keyword occurrence on a competitor page is a qualitative relevance signal, not proof of monthly search demand, difficulty or that page's Google ranking.
