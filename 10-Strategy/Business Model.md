# Business Model

## Working Model
The side company acquires customers independently, sells approved Twin Disc products through its own website/RFQ process, and purchases fulfilled items from MarIndustrial under an agreed reseller arrangement.

## Working Pricing Assumption
If reseller cost is approximately 60% of retail/list price, initial web pricing can be tested around 80–90% of retail rather than automatically using one discount for every SKU.

Examples before fees/shipping/returns:
- Sell at 90% of retail → gross margin about 33.3% when cost is 60% of retail.
- Sell at 85% of retail → gross margin about 29.4%.
- Sell at 80% of retail → gross margin 25%.

## Must Be Confirmed Before Launch
- Written reseller/supplier approval.
- Exact discount structure by item/manufacturer.
- Whether public advertising of Twin Disc products/pricing is allowed.
- Trademark, image, and documentation permissions.
- Warranty and returns responsibility.
- Drop-shipping vs reseller receipt.
- Payment and credit terms.
- Shipping, customs, duties, and tax responsibilities.
- Permitted use of SAP-derived aggregate intelligence.

## Data Boundary
Existing MarIndustrial customer lists, transaction-level customer data, negotiated costs, and other confidential information are not to be published or used outside approved purposes. Raw SAP data stays local/private; GitHub stores query definitions, schemas, sanitized outputs, and code.
