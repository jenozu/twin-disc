# SEO-02 — Editorial Governance and Quality Gates
**DRAFT POLICY — Human and technical approval still required.**
Every page must solve one evidenced buyer question; use accurate Twin Disc family/SKU nomenclature, correct exact variant/BOM and units/revisions; distinguish sourced fact, family application, owner claim, customer-report and hypothesis. Include useful original tables, safe identification checklist and descriptive next action. Evidence citations use source URL, revision, applicable model and page/table where available; copyrighted drawings/manuals/photos need permission or link instead of republication.

AI may create outline, natural-language synthesis, SEO metadata, links and comparison layout from approved claim IDs, but must not invent specifications, product interchange, direct drop-ins, discontinued status, repair adjustments, safety instructions, maintenance intervals, stock, prices, genuine case studies, technician qualifications, local office, authorization or emergency service. No mass near-identical location pages, no unreviewed mechanical advice; missing fields remain explicit gaps. Do not include raw SAP confidential customer, supplier cost, order, inventory, private BOM or PII.

**Page-level release checklist** (ALL applicable must pass):
- [ ] Unique intent/canonical and helpful original substance (no near duplicate).
- [ ] Complete correct heading hierarchy, title/description, accessibility/alt and mobile copy.
- [ ] Verified model/BOM context, revision, units, claim-source provenance and source rights.
- [ ] Phase4 Yes/Exact or qualified Family-only; Review/HOLD/No not marketed as exact equivalents.
- [ ] Qualified named technical reviewer signs technical/safety claims.
- [ ] Owner signs seller identity, service geography/capabilities, RFQ response, privacy, asset rights, fulfillment, warranty/returns and commercial text.
- [ ] Structured data, search aliases and cards do not leak prohibited claims; no fake price, review, stock, Offer.
- [ ] Parent/internal links verified, no dead links and safe conversion fallback.
- [ ] lastVerifiedAt, nextReviewAt and change history stored, with a clear responsible editor.
Approval cannot be generated or signed by an LLM.

**Maintenance:** recheck exact SKU/OEM and supply wording quarterly; explanatory guides semiannually; revalidate immediately after manual revision, manufacturer notice, supersession/failed fulfillment, contradiction, rights challenge or a broken supporting source. Revoke affected claims/links pending review and reapprove explicitly. Retire truly redundant URLs with proper equivalence-tested 301; 410 genuinely removed content with no equivalent. Maintain decision log, prior slug, impacted links, new source revision and reviewer. Source freshness is separate from static site build time.
